03Savoir-faire

Anatomy of a takchita

Three hundred and twenty hours, five pairs of hands, one piece. Follow the needle through Lalla Noor, our couture takchita.

  1. 01

    Tarz el-mjboud

    Gold thread couched by hand over cotton cord until each motif stands in relief — a Fassi technique handed down from mâalma to apprentice.

  2. 02

    The mdamma

    A filigree belt, pierced and gilded by hand, gathers both layers into a single line at the waist.

  3. 03

    The sleeves

    The wide sleeves carry the heaviest embroidery — ninety hours alone, worked on a wooden frame so the chiffon never puckers.

  4. 04

    Sfifa

    A braid of silk and gold, plaited on the fingers and then stitched along every edge to frame the silhouette.

  5. 05

    Dfina & tahtiya

    Two layers worn as one: a velvet tahtiya beneath, a sheer chiffon dfina above, so the green deepens with every step.
