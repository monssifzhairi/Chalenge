01Our story

ⵜⵉⵣⵉⵔⵉ

Moonlight, by hand

Tiziri means moonlight in Tamazight — the light by which, in our grandmothers’ houses, the last stitches of a wedding kaftan were finished on the roof terrace. We took the name in 2009, when three artisans and one embroidery frame moved into a crumbling riad off the Talaa Kebira.

Seventeen years later, forty-two maâlems and apprentices work in that same riad. Each piece still passes through the same hands: the cutter, the sfifa braider, the embroiderer, the button knotter, the finisher. Nothing is outsourced, nothing is rushed, and every piece carries a small label signed by the maâlem who completed it.

We make clothes for the moments that matter — a henna night, an Eid morning, a first winter in a new city — and for all the ordinary days in between. Pieces meant to be worn hard, altered, repaired, and one day inherited.

“We are not making costumes for the past. We are making the clothes our daughters will fight over.”

Kenza B., creative director

02Savoir-faire

Four gestures, centuries old

The techniques of the Moroccan couture tradition are taught hand to hand, never written down. These are the four you will find in every Tiziri piece.

  1. 01

    Sfifa

    A flat braid of silk — sometimes gold — plaited by hand around the fingers, then stitched along every seam and edge. One kaftan can take forty metres.

    Seen on Kaftan Yasmine
  2. 02

    Aakad

    Tiny buttons knotted from silk thread, one by one, each with its own loop. A skilled maâlma ties forty in an hour; a kaftan may need a hundred.

    Seen on Jabador Sidi
  3. 03

    Tarz el-mjboud

    Gold thread laid over cotton cord and couched down by hand, so the motif rises from the velvet. The signature of Fassi wedding couture.

    Seen on Takchita Lalla Noor
  4. 04

    Silver threadwork

    Needle embroidery in silver thread, its patterns traced from the carved gebs plaster of our riad — the motif of the Clair de Lune collection.

    Seen on Kaftan Layl

03Milestones

Seventeen years

  1. 2009

    A riad in Fès el-Bali

    Three artisans, one embroidery frame and a riad off the Talaa Kebira, restored room by room.

  2. 2012

    The first takchita

    A couture takchita for a Fassi wedding — and a waiting list that began by word of mouth.

  3. 2016

    Marrakech, Guéliz

    Our first boutique opens on Rue de la Liberté, with a fitting salon for made-to-measure.

  4. 2019

    Casablanca & the men’s line

    A boutique on Boulevard d’Anfa, and djellabas woven for us on the looms of Ouezzane.

  5. 2022

    Rabat, Agdal

    A third boutique — and fittings by video call for clients from Paris to Montréal.

  6. 2026

    Clair de Lune

    Forty-two maâlems, and a collection inspired by our riad after dark.

Our commitments

Made slowly

Every piece is made by hand, in small runs or to order. Our maâlems are paid by the hour, never by the piece.

Rooted in Morocco

Wool from Ouezzane, leather from the Chouara tannery, silver and brass from the souks of Fès. Designed and made entirely in Morocco.

Made to last

Alterations are free for life in our boutiques, and any piece can return to the atelier for repair.

04Sur mesure

Made to measure

Any piece of the collection — or a design of your own — cut to your measurements, in the fabric, colour and embroidery you choose.

  1. 01

    The consultation

    In boutique or by video call. We take twenty-four measurements and choose fabric, colour and embroidery with you.

  2. 02

    Two fittings

    While the atelier cuts and embroiders, we fit the piece twice — in person or on camera — to perfect every line.

  3. 03

    The delivery

    Hand-delivered in Morocco, or shipped worldwide by DHL Express in a cedar-scented garment box.

Made to measure from 4,500 MAD · allow four to eight weeks.
