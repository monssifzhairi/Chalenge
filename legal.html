01Privacy

Privacy policy

Maison Tiziri, whose atelier is located on the Talaa Kebira, Fès el-Bali, Morocco, is responsible for the personal data collected through this website, our boutiques and our messaging channels. We process it in accordance with Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, under the supervision of the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel).

What we collect

  • The details you give us when you order or contact us: name, phone number, email, delivery address and the content of your messages.
  • Your measurements, if you entrust us with a made-to-measure piece.
  • Your email address, if you subscribe to our letters.

Why we use it

To confirm, prepare and deliver your orders, to answer your questions, to make your made-to-measure pieces and — only with your consent — to send you our letters. We never sell or rent your data.

Who receives it

Only the partners strictly necessary to serve you: our delivery companies, our payment provider (CMI) for card payments, and WhatsApp (Meta) when you choose to contact us there.

How long we keep it

Order data is kept for the duration required by our accounting obligations; your measurements for as long as you remain a client, unless you ask us to delete them; your email until you unsubscribe — a link is included in every letter.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and object to the processing of your data. To exercise it, write to {{email}}; we reply within 30 days.

02Terms

Terms of sale

1. Scope

These terms apply to all purchases made from Maison Tiziri through this website, WhatsApp, telephone or email, by consumers in Morocco and abroad.

2. Prices

Prices are shown in Moroccan dirham (MAD), all taxes included, excluding delivery costs, which are indicated before your order is confirmed. Prices applicable are those displayed on the day of the order.

3. Orders

Your selection is sent to our concierge, who confirms availability, delivery and the total amount with you. The sale is concluded once you have confirmed this summary. Made-to-order and made-to-measure pieces are confirmed upon payment of a 50% deposit.

4. Payment

In Morocco, orders are paid on delivery in cash or by card, or in advance via a secure CMI payment link. International orders are paid in advance by secure card link.

5. Delivery

Delivery times and costs are set out on our delivery page. Any visible damage should be reported to the courier on receipt and to our concierge within 48 hours.

6. Right of withdrawal, returns & exchanges

Law No. 31-08 on consumer protection grants a 7-day right of withdrawal for distance purchases. Maison Tiziri extends this period to 14 days from delivery for ready-to-wear pieces, under the conditions set out on our returns page. Made-to-order, made-to-measure and altered pieces, being made to your specifications, are excluded.

7. Warranty

All our pieces benefit from the legal warranty against hidden defects. Any manufacturing defect will be repaired or the piece replaced at no cost to you.

8. Disputes

These terms are governed by Moroccan law. In the event of a disagreement, we will always look for an amicable solution first; failing that, the competent courts of Fès shall have jurisdiction, subject to the consumer’s statutory rights.
