02Good to know

Frequently asked

How do I place an order?

Add your pieces to the bag and tap “Order via WhatsApp”: your order is sent to our concierge, who confirms availability, delivery and payment with you personally. You can also order by phone or in boutique.

Do you deliver outside Morocco?

Yes — we ship worldwide by DHL Express in 4 to 8 working days. International orders are paid in advance by secure card link. See delivery rates.

Can I try a piece before buying?

Of course. Every piece of the collection can be tried in our boutiques in Marrakech, Casablanca and Rabat. In Morocco, ready-to-wear pieces can also be exchanged within 14 days.

How long does made to measure take?

Allow four to eight weeks depending on the embroidery, with two fittings in between. For weddings, we recommend contacting us three months ahead. How it works.
