    Size guide

    How to measure

    • Bust / chest — around the fullest part, keeping the tape horizontal.
    • Waist — around the natural waistline, the narrowest part of the torso.
    • Hips — around the fullest part, feet together.
    • Foot length — heel to longest toe, standing, at the end of the day.
