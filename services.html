03Returns & exchanges

Fourteen days to be sure

Ready-to-wear pieces can be exchanged or returned within 14 days of delivery, unworn, with their label attached and in their original packaging. In Morocco, exchanges are free: we collect the piece at your door.

  1. 01

    Message us on WhatsApp or by email with your name and the piece concerned.

  2. 02

    We collect the piece at your address within 48 hours, or you drop it in a boutique.

  3. 03

    Exchange or refund — your new size ships immediately; refunds are made by bank transfer within 7 days of receipt.

Made-to-order, made-to-measure and altered pieces are made especially for you and cannot be returned. Every piece remains covered for manufacturing defects.

04Size guide

Find your size

Body measurements, not garment measurements. Between two sizes, or wearing a piece for a special occasion? Our stylists will gladly advise you, and alterations are free in our boutiques.

How to measure

  • Bust / chest — around the fullest part, keeping the tape horizontal.
  • Waist — around the natural waistline, the narrowest part of the torso.
  • Hips — around the fullest part, feet together.
  • Foot length — heel to longest toe, standing, at the end of the day.

05Garment care

Made to last — with a little care

Silk & satin

Dry clean only. Steam from the inside on a low setting and store on a padded hanger, away from light.

Velvet & embroidery

Professional dry clean. Never iron embroidery; hang inside a breathable cotton cover.

Wool

Brush after wearing and air between wears. Store folded, with a block of cedar against moths.

Linen & cotton

Machine wash cold, inside out, and line dry. Iron on the reverse while slightly damp.

Leather

Wipe with a soft dry cloth and nourish with a neutral cream. Keep away from water and heat.

Aftercare for life

Alterations are free in our boutiques, and any piece can return to the atelier for repair.

06FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add your pieces to the bag and tap “Order via WhatsApp”. Your order arrives with our concierge, who confirms availability, delivery and payment with you personally — usually within the hour. You can also order by phone or in boutique.

Is payment on delivery available everywhere in Morocco?

Yes. Cash and card on delivery are available in every city served by our courier partners, which covers the whole of Morocco.

Can I change or cancel my order?

As long as it hasn’t left the atelier, simply send us a message on WhatsApp. Made-to-order pieces can be cancelled within 24 hours of the deposit.

How do I know which size to choose?

Use our size guide, and don’t hesitate to send your measurements to a stylist on WhatsApp — we will recommend the right size for the piece and the occasion.

Do you offer alterations?

Yes, free of charge and for life in our boutiques in Marrakech, Casablanca and Rabat. Outside these cities, send the piece back to the atelier and we cover the return delivery in Morocco.

Are your pieces really made by hand?

Entirely. Every piece is cut, embroidered and finished in our atelier in the Fès medina, and carries a label signed by the maâlem who completed it. Our babouches are made by a family workshop near the Chouara tannery.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Every order arrives in our signature box with a handwritten card. To add a personal message or hide the price, just tell us when you confirm your order.

How long does made to measure take?

Four to eight weeks depending on the embroidery, with two fittings in between. For weddings, we recommend contacting us three months ahead. Discover the process.
